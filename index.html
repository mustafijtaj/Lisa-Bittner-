About the Book

Some places find you when you need them most.

Ava Carter has spent years being the woman everyone relies on — strong, capable, and always in control. But beneath her polished life, she is quietly exhausted and searching for something she can no longer name. Hoping to escape the noise of the city and the weight of her past, she retreats to a secluded cabin in the Smoky Mountains.

What she does not expect is Luke Walker.

When a booking mistake forces Ava and Luke to share the same cabin, two guarded strangers are drawn into an unexpected connection. Surrounded by mountain trails, quiet evenings, and flickering-lit nights, Ava begins to discover that healing can come softly.

One cabin. Two guarded hearts. A season that changes everything.

Meet the Author

Lisa Bittner

Lisa Bittner is a storyteller at heart who believes every life holds a story worth sharing. Inspired by life's unexpected turns, the beauty of the world around her, and the power of love, hope, and second chances, Lisa writes stories that touch the heart.

She finds inspiration in her Christian faith, the beauty of the mountains, quiet moments with a cup of coffee, and the meaningful connections that make life special.

Through her books, Lisa hopes to leave readers with a little more hope, a little more love, and a reminder that the best chapters may still be waiting to be written.
